Our beginning

Born from a
traveller's promise


Dreamcatcher began with a wish — to build a place where the mountains feel like home. Not a hotel. Not a resort. Something older and warmer than that. A dwelling where the door is always open and the chai is always on.


Perched above McLeod Ganj in the mist-wrapped village of Dharamkot, we sit at the edge of the Dhauladhar range — close enough to touch the clouds, far enough from the world to breathe again.

"We spread love, not rules. Come as a stranger. Leave as someone who remembers."

Beyond the door

The mountains
are calling

Dharamkot sits at the gateway to some of Himachal's most breathtaking trails. We know every path, every viewpoint, every campfire worth sitting around.

01

Triund Trek

The classic. 9km of pine forest and open ridgeline ending at a meadow that makes you forget every city you've ever lived in. The Dhauladhar range, right there, filling the horizon.

9km Moderate 4-5 hours Day hike
02

Overnight Camping

Sleep at Triund top under a sky so full of stars it feels like a different planet. Bonfire, blankets, and a sunrise that will stay with you for years. Some nights change you.

Overnight All levels Guided Bonfire
03

Indrahar Pass

For the serious trekker. At 4,342m above sea level, this is one of Himachal's great crossings — over the Dhauladhar into the Kangra Valley. Two to three days. Not for the faint of heart.

Advanced 2-3 days 4,342m Guided
04

Bhagsu Waterfall

An easy, beautiful 30-minute walk through Bhagsu village to a cascading waterfall. Perfect for a slow morning when the mountains are calling but your legs are asking for something gentler.

Easy 30 minutes Year-round Solo-friendly

Find your way here

Getting to
Dreamcatcher

From Delhi

Overnight Volvo bus to McLeod Ganj (~12 hours, ₹600-900). From the bus stand, take an auto to Dharamkot for ₹100 — or walk uphill 20 minutes through the village. Both are beautiful.

From Dharamshala Airport

Taxi to Dharamkot takes about 45 minutes (₹500-700). Tell the driver Dreamcatcher Homestay — everyone in the area knows us.

From McLeod Ganj

A 20-minute walk uphill through Dharamkot village, or a ₹100 auto. Once you reach Dharamkot, ask any local. You'll find us.

Before you write to us

Questions wanderers
often ask

Do you provide meals with the room?+
Meals are not included in the room price, but the Dreamcatcher Cafe is right next door serving Indian and Middle Eastern food all day — breakfast to dinner, plus chai whenever you need it.
Is it safe for solo female travellers?+
Yes. Many of our guests are solo women travellers. Dharamkot is a quiet, community-watched village, and our common spaces make it easy to meet other travellers within hours of arriving.
Can you arrange the Triund trek or camping for us?+
Absolutely — just mention it in your booking message or ask on WhatsApp. We know local guides personally and can arrange day treks, overnight camping, or the longer Indrahar Pass crossing.
What if I need to cancel or change my dates?+
Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can — we understand travel plans change and we'll always try to work something out with you directly. No rigid cancellation forms here.
Is there wifi and hot water?+
Yes to both. Wifi covers the common areas and most rooms, and hot water is available throughout the day — mountain mornings get cold, we make sure you're comfortable.
